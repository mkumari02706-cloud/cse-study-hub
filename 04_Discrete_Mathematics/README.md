## 📋 Topic Checklist

### Unit 1: Propositional Logic & Predicate Calculus
- [ ] Propositions, Truth Tables, Logical Connectives (Negation, Conjunction, Disjunction, Implication, Biconditional)
- [ ] Tautology, Contradiction, Contingency, Logical Equivalence laws
- [ ] Predicates and Quantifiers (Universal $\forall$, Existential $\exists$, Negation of quantified statements)
- [ ] Normal Forms: Conjunctive Normal Form (CNF), Disjunctive Normal Form (DNF)
- [ ] Rules of Inference & Valid Arguments (Modus Ponens, Modus Tollens, Resolution)

### Unit 2: Proof Techniques & Mathematical Induction
- [ ] Methods of Proof: Direct proof, Proof by Contraposition, Proof by Contradiction
- [ ] Counterexamples and Exhaustive Proofs
- [ ] Principle of Mathematical Induction (Weak Induction)
- [ ] Principle of Strong Mathematical Induction
- [ ] Well-Ordering Principle

### Unit 3: Set Theory, Relations & Functions
- [ ] Sets, Subsets, Power Sets, Cartesian Products, Venn Diagrams
- [ ] Set Identities & De Morgan's Laws
- [ ] Relations: Reflexive, Irreflexive, Symmetric, Antisymmetric, Transitive
- [ ] Equivalence Relations, Equivalence Classes, and Partitions
- [ ] Partial Orderings: Posets, Hasse Diagrams, Maximal/Minimal/Greatest/Least elements
- [ ] Lattices: Meet ($\wedge$), Join ($\vee$), Bounded, Complemented, and Distributive Lattices
- [ ] Functions: Injective (one-to-one), Surjective (onto), Bijective, Inverse, and Composition of functions

### Unit 4: Combinatorics & Counting Principles
- [ ] Basic Counting: Rule of Sum, Rule of Product
- [ ] Pigeonhole Principle (PHP) and Generalized Pigeonhole Principle
- [ ] Permutations and Combinations (with and without repetition)
- [ ] Binomial Theorem and Pascal's Identity
- [ ] Principle of Inclusion-Exclusion (PIE) & Derangements ($D_n$)
- [ ] Recurrence Relations: Solving Linear Homogeneous Recurrences with constant coefficients
- [ ] Solving Non-Homogeneous Recurrence Relations
- [ ] Generating Functions basics

### Unit 5: Graph Theory
- [ ] Graph definitions: Vertices, Edges, Degrees, Handshaking Lemma
- [ ] Special Graphs: Complete ($K_n$), Bipartite ($K_{m,n}$), Cycles ($C_n$), Paths ($P_n$)
- [ ] Graph Isomorphism and Subgraphs
- [ ] Paths, Cycles, Connected Components, Cut vertices, and Bridges
- [ ] Eulerian Paths and Circuits (Euler's Theorem)
- [ ] Hamiltonian Paths and Circuits (Dirac's and Ore's Theorems)
- [ ] Planar Graphs & Euler's Formula ($V - E + F = 2$), Kuratowski's Theorem
- [ ] Graph Coloring: Chromatic Number, Four Color Theorem
- [ ] Trees: Properties, Rooted trees, Spanning trees, Minimum Spanning Trees

### Unit 6: Algebraic Structures & Group Theory
- [ ] Binary Operations and Algebraic Systems
- [ ] Semigroups, Monoids, and Groups (Closure, Associativity, Identity, Inverse)
- [ ] Abelian Groups and Finite Groups
- [ ] Subgroups and Criteria for Subgroups
- [ ] Cyclic Groups, Generators, and Orders of elements
- [ ] Cosets and Lagrange's Theorem for finite groups
- [ ] Normal Subgroups, Quotient Groups, and Homomorphisms overview
- [ ] Rings, Integral Domains, and Fields definitions

### Unit 7: Boolean Algebra
- [ ] Axiomatic definition of Boolean Algebra
- [ ] Duality Principle and Boolean Algebra Laws
- [ ] Boolean Expressions, Functions, and Minterms/Maxterms representation
- [ ] Logic Gates representation and Circuit minimization using Boolean algebra
